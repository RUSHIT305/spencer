# Code of Conduct

Spencer is a professional project. Contributors and users are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and implementation rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, malicious disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable. Project maintainers may remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe and productive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers rather than escalating them in public threads. Include enough context to investigate, while avoiding personal data and secrets.
